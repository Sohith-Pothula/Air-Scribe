How it works

Hand tracking runs entirely in your browser. The server only sees a small black-and-white image of your strokes.

  1. 01

    Track

    MediaPipe finds 21 points on your hand every frame, right in the browser using WebAssembly.

  2. 02

    Gesture

    Each finger counts as "up" when its tip is farther from the wrist than its middle joint. The combination picks the mode.

  3. 03

    Draw

    Your fingertip is smoothed and debounced, then drawn as curves so lines stay clean even with shaky hands.

  4. 04

    Read

    The strokes go to the FastAPI backend. Gemini reads the word and its meaning, or an offline CNN reads a digit.
